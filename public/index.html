BEGINS HERE.

In 2024, a private energy company called OMEGA is testing a breakthrough clean-energy system. When a field trial goes wrong outside Windhoek, an ordinary rescue driver uncovers a cover-up reaching from Namibia's cities to a remote Arctic research station and an international lunar research mission. Explore, work, farm, build a life, protect your friends and decide what happens next.

Chapter One · The First Signal

THE YEAR IS 2024 · NAMIBIA

After an OMEGA energy-test convoy disappears on a remote road outside Windhoek, Ricardo Haindaka, a skilled rescue driver, finds an emergency beacon carrying a recording that was never meant to leave the company. Zara, a Namibian pilot, and Drew, an engineer who helped build the test equipment, join the investigation. Their search leads through familiar city streets, farms, coastal ports and a research station in the Arctic. The world is recognisable; the technology is only a little ahead of ours.
